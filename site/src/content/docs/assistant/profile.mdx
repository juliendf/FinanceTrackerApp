The Profile page is not an account. It is the brief the assistant reads before answering,
plus the list of facts it is allowed to remember.

## Identity

First name is required; last name is optional. Nothing else on the page is required — the
assistant simply has less to work with.

## Financial context

| Field | Why the assistant wants it |
|---|---|
| Currency | The currency of the amounts below |
| Job Title | Tone and relevance of advice |
| Annual Salary | Savings rate, capacity to invest |
| Monthly Living Expenses | Runway, savings rate, goal feasibility. The field says it plainly: this is what makes those computable |
| Monthly Savings Target | Treated as an intention, not a measurement |
| Date of Birth | Age-aware advice; the form shows the computed age |
| Employment | A variable income needs a longer buffer |
| Dependants and their ages | Education costs, insurance, horizon |
| Marital Status and Matrimonial Regime | Succession and property questions |
| Will | *No* is an action item; *not stated* is a question the assistant will ask |
| Country of Tax Residence | Jurisdiction-aware answers |
| Risk Profile | Conservative, Moderate, Growth, Aggressive |
| Other Aspirations | Free text, shared alongside the structured goals |

Amounts are validated like every other money field: at most 2 decimals and 10,000,000.

## Response style

Three settings that are pasted into the prompt verbatim:

| Setting | Options |
|---|---|
| **AI Tone** | Encouraging · Neutral · Direct |
| **AI Verbosity** | Concise · Balanced · Detailed |
| **Financial Expertise** | Beginner · Intermediate · Expert |

Defaults are Encouraging, Balanced, Intermediate. Changing them takes effect on the next
message, not on old answers.

## Financial goals

Structured goals, listed in the prompt as `label — amount currency by date (priority,
category)`. Each goal has a label, an optional target amount and date, a priority (low,
medium, high) and a category (Retirement, Housing, Education, Travel, Emergency Fund, Debt
Payoff, Other).

They are what makes questions like *"am I on track?"* answerable with arithmetic instead of
vibes. Empty goals produce a generic answer, and the assistant will usually say so.

## Memories

**What I know about you** is a table of facts, each with a category (Personal, Financial,
Goal, Preference, Life Event), an importance from 1 to 5, and a source:

- **AI** — proposed in chat, landed here because you confirmed the card.
- **Manual** — added with **Add memory**.

The **ten with the highest importance** are included in every prompt. Everything else is
stored but not shown to the model, so an important fact buried at importance 2 is
effectively invisible. Raise its importance instead of repeating it in chat.

Write memories in the third person, as facts: *"User has two children, ages 6 and 9"*,
*"User keeps six months of expenses in CHF cash"*. Editing is allowed and keeps the
memory's identity; deleting asks for confirmation.

:::caution[Everything here is sent to the provider]
Profile facts, goals, memories and standing instructions are part of every request. Keep
them to what you are comfortable sending to the endpoint you configured — or use a local
model.
:::
